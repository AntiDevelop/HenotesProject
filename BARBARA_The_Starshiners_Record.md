# BARBARA: THE STARSHINERS RECORD

*An analog-horror / found-footage adaptation of* **BARBARA** *by Thananarkin Ekabut*

Adapted in the style of *Noroi: The Curse* and *The Mandela Catalogue*: a "recovered" documentary built from tapes, phone recordings, broadcasts, interviews and records. The viewer has to put the clues together and work out what is coming.

---

## FORMAT NOTES (read before the script)

- **Three kinds of footage are cut together**, and each has its own look:
  1. **RUTH'S DOCUMENTARY** (2025): clean digital, handheld, timecode burned in at the bottom right (`TC 01:14:22`). Shot by TEO.
  2. **RECOVERED MEDIA**: VHS (tracking lines, a date stamp at the bottom left), public-access TV, voicemails, phone call recordings, phone video, Instagram screen captures.
  3. **THE CARTOON**: *Tales of Olympus, Vol. 3: "Barbaros and the Star"*, a cheap early-90s cel-animated VHS for children. Bright colors, soft music, a warm narrator. It should always feel a little wrong.
- **Title cards** are white Helvetica on black, dry and factual like a documentary. They are written by JOAN CALLOWAY, the editor.
- **Hidden frames.** Single frames are spliced into the footage (see `CLUE_BIBLE.md`). The script marks them with **[FRAME]**. They should be almost subliminal: one or two frames long.
- **The rule of the cult:** *a name that is given can be taken.* Every time a character says their full name aloud, writes it, or signs it, it matters. Watch for it.

## COLD OPEN

**VHS. DATE STAMP: APR 11 1986  6:52 AM**

**EXT. HALVORSEN FARM - WENTWORTH, NEW HAMPSHIRE - DAWN**

An overcast sky. Grey fields. Sheep can be heard somewhere off-camera. The camera wobbles badly; it is heavy, shoulder-mounted, and held by someone who doesn't usually film.

DR. GERALD FISK (V.O.)
*(clearing throat)*
This is, uh... April eleventh. Halvorsen place, out on Atwell Hill. Ewe in distress, owner says since about four a.m. For the file.

ARNE HALVORSEN (60s), in a flannel shirt and bare-headed, walks ahead of the camera toward the barn.

ARNE
She was fine Wednesday. Fine. Black one. Only black one I got.

**INT. HALVORSEN BARN - CONTINUOUS**

Dim. Dust in the light from the door. A BLACK EWE lies on her side in the straw. Her belly is swollen far past any normal pregnancy, tight as a drum.

The camera pans right. Fourteen WHITE SHEEP stand in a perfectly straight row along the far wall. All of them face the black ewe. None of them move, chew, or bleat.

DR. FISK (V.O.)
Arne. Why are the others lined up like that?

ARNE
That's the thing. They been like that since I come in. Won't eat. Won't do nothing. Just watching her.

The camera moves closer to the ewe. Something **shifts** under the skin of her belly. It isn't kicking. It is a slow, deliberate movement, like a hand smoothing out a sheet.

The ewe moans. It is a low, wet, almost human sound.

DR. FISK (V.O.)
Okay. Okay, girl. I'm gonna...

He sets the camera down on a hay bale. The frame is crooked. We see the ewe, and the white sheep in their row behind her.

Fisk's gloved hands come into frame. The ewe's eye rolls toward the lens.

She **screams.** It goes on far too long.

Her belly **splits**.

The camera's auto-iris goes white for a moment, then recovers.

Something stands up out of the ewe. It is a **lamb**. It is fully formed, perfectly white, and clean, with no blood or fluid on it. It stands on steady legs, as if it has been standing for years.

It walks calmly past the dead ewe and takes the empty place at the end of the row.

All fifteen white sheep **turn their heads toward the camera at the same time.**

DR. FISK (O.S.)
*(barely audible)*
That's not hers. Arne, that's not... that isn't *hers.*

The tape cuts to snow.

**[FRAME]**: *a hand-drawn cartoon sheep, bloated, black, smiling.*

CUT TO BLACK.

**INT. PRUITT HOUSE - LIVING ROOM - DAY**

An ordinary suburban living room in Concord. KATELYN "KATIE" PRUITT (14) sits on the couch with her knees pulled up. Her mother, LISA PRUITT (40s), sits next to her, protective and uneasy.

When Katie speaks, her voice is **unnaturally deep and muffled**. It is the same voice the original script gives Barbara.

RUTH (O.S.)
Can you tell me how you and Barbara met?

KATIE
Speech therapy. At the Bow clinic. We were, like, nine. We both have... this.
*(she touches her throat)*
It's a thing with our vocal folds. There's a long name. Barbara's was worse. Kids used to do, like, a monster voice at us.

LISA
They still do.

KATIE
So we were the freaks together. That was our whole thing. "Freak club."
*(a small, sad laugh)*

RUTH (O.S.)
What happened in October?

KATIE
She had a presentation in English and she... she kind of collapsed. Like, a panic attack. In front of everybody. And after that she stopped texting me. And then she wasn't at school for like a week.

RUTH (O.S.)
And then she came back.

KATIE
*(long pause)*
Then Zora came.

She holds up her phone toward the lens.

**INSERT - PHONE SCREEN (INSTAGRAM)**

A profile: **@zora.shines**. **312K followers.** The profile picture shows a girl of about fourteen who is unbelievably beautiful. Her skin is flawless and her face is perfectly symmetrical. She smiles with her head tilted exactly the right amount.

KATIE (V.O.)
She just showed up. Nobody knew where she came from. Everyone's obsessed with her. And she sits in Barbara's seat. In every class. Barbara's seat.

RUTH (O.S.)
Did you ask the school?

KATIE
I asked Ms. Weller where Barbara went. She looked at me like I was being weird and said Barbara was right there.
*(she points, as if the girl were in the room)*
She pointed at *Zora.*

Katie's mother looks at the floor.

LISA
I called Diane. Barbara's mom. She said Barbara's doing great. Better than ever. She said...
*(hesitates)*
She said, "She's finally happy, Lisa. Let her be happy."

RUTH (O.S.)
Katie, what makes you sure it isn't Barbara? Kids change. People get glow-ups.

KATIE
*(right away, with certainty)*
Because she hugged me.

Silence.

KATIE (CONT'D)
At lunch. I was crying about Barbara, like an idiot, to *Zora*, and she hugged me. And she hugs like Barbara. Barbara puts her chin on your shoulder and like, presses down, like she's trying to hold you still. Nobody else does that.
*(her voice breaks)*
And she said "I'm sorry." Not like "sorry for your loss." Like *she* did something.

**EXT. ALLEY - DAY (ANIMATED)**

Perfectly beautiful FLOWERS are everywhere. Wedged into the shadowed corner of an alley is BARBAROS (32). She is the only character drawn in a **different style**: a rougher line, muddy colors, a face that is a little "off-model," as if another animator drew her.

BARBAROS

Barbaros gazes at the sky. A spark grows, brighter and brighter.

HENOTES

BARBAROS
Oh!! Ahhh!!!!

## PART THREE: THE CALL

**DIGITAL. TC 00:21:03**

**INT. RUTH'S APARTMENT - NIGHT**

Teo has the cracked phone plugged into a laptop.

TEO
It's got one of those auto call-recorder apps. Free one. Records everything. There's one call. October nineteenth, 9:47 p.m. Eleven minutes.

RUTH
Play it.

**PHONE RECORDING. Waveform on screen. Subtitled.**

The audio starts with fumbling sounds and breathing. Then:

BARBARA (V.O.)
*(breathing heavily, deep voice)*
Alright. It's going to be alright.

A dial tone. Seven tones, which is wrong for a phone number: the number has only nine digits and no area code a real phone would accept. It rings once. Twice.

The line clicks.

EMMA (V.O.)
*(perfect voice, perfect pace; robotically perfect but still friendly and warm)*
You have reached the Starshiners Society! Are you seeking help with your body?

A long silence. Then a thud, as the phone hits the carpet. Barbara is sobbing somewhere far from the mic.

EMMA (V.O.)
No. No. It's okay. Take your time. We're already glad you even reached out to us.

Rustling. Barbara picks the phone back up.

BARBARA (V.O.)
Yes. I want your help.

EMMA (V.O.)
Perfect! We're proud of you for taking such an initiative! Alright, can you tell us how you feel about yourself? This is a free space to open up.

Silence. Four seconds.

BARBARA (V.O.)
I wish I wasn't who I am.

EMMA (V.O.)
Why do you say that?

BARBARA (V.O.)
This world is only meant for those who are born- who- who actually belong here. But you see, it's only me... In the entire room, the entire school, the entire universe for all I care... It's only me who's the fucking alien. It's only me. It's me. IT'S JUST ME. Don't- Don't you hear my voice? Would you expect this kind of voice from a little girl? Would you!? You know the truth. You know it.

EMMA (V.O.)
It's totally understandable to feel that way. And that's what we're here for. Listen to me. Abandon those painful memories about your body. We will fix it, so you will become the beautiful star you're always supposed to be. And you, you will finally belong in this world. In harmony with everything. Because nobody deserves to feel foreign. Nobody.

BARBARA (V.O.)
I don't know what to say. Thank you so much. I can't believe you're actually going to help me.

EMMA (V.O.)
There's only one more step to take. We're a small community working offline, so you'll have to come to our place. If you can, our people will happily welcome you. It's free, and we're open all the time.
*(beat)*
Before you go, can I have your name, sweetheart? Your full name. Say it nice and clear.

BARBARA (V.O.)
Barbara. Barbara Ann Nowak.

EMMA (V.O.)
*(a soft intake of breath, almost pleasure)*
Thank you, Barbara. We have it now.

BARBARA (V.O.)
*(weeping)*
Thank you so much. God is good, after all. God is good. God is-

The line goes **dead**. But the recording doesn't stop. It keeps running for **nine more minutes**.

TEO (O.S.)
She hung up. Why's it still going?

He drags the playhead forward. Silence, silence... At 8:12 there is a sound, very faint. Teo boosts the gain.

It's Emma's voice. She is no longer talking to Barbara. She is repeating, slowly and lovingly, in a monotone:

EMMA (V.O.)
*(distant, looping)*
...Barbara Ann Nowak. Barbara Ann Nowak. Barbara Ann Nowak. Barbara Ann...

Under her voice, more and more voices slowly join in, saying the name with her. Ten voices. Fifty. A crowd. All perfectly in time.

Ruth reaches over and stops the playback. Neither of them speaks.

## PART SIX: THE SCHOLAR

**VHS. DATE STAMP: OCT 22 1998. Camcorder on a tripod at the back of a church hall.**

**INT. GREEK ORTHODOX CHURCH HALL - LOWELL, MA - NIGHT**

Folding chairs. Twenty elderly attendees. DR. ADAM PETRAKIS (52) stands at a slide projector. He is intense and a little embarrassed by how seriously he takes this.

ADAM
And to Henotes, the so-called Greek "patron of beauty," our clan never spoke of him as divine. In fact, he is the contrary of divinity. Take a look.
